Question screen

A clickable prototype of how the KETGO Question screen behaves. App text is Lithuanian, as in the app.

Desktop (a wide window): the question, a large picture and, after Check, the "why" sit on the left. The Options and buttons sit on the right. Keys 1–4 tick an Option and Enter checks. In the Mock Exam, all 30 numbers sit next to the buttons as an answer sheet. Use "Phone view" to see the phone.

A, B and C are the phone layouts. They differ only in where the explanation appears after Check. Switch while an answer is showing to compare.

  • A Below: marks on the Options; verdict and key sentence in the bottom bar; explanation under the Options.
  • B Sheet: a panel at the bottom holds the verdict and explanation. The marked Options stay above it.
  • C Above: verdict and explanation sit between the question and the Options.

Try this

  1. Question 1 is a new Rule. Guess, or tap "Pirma išmokti" to read the Rule first.
  2. Answer one Question with "Spėju" (not sure): the explanation stays open. Answer sure and right: it folds away.
  3. Get Question 3 (U-turns) wrong. It comes back two Questions later with other Options.
  4. Open the Mock Exam: skip, go back, finish early, try "Be laiko ribos", or fast-forward the timer.

The Lesson end and Mock Exam results screens are placeholders: they are designed during the build.
