Question screen

A clickable prototype of how the KETGO Question screen behaves. App text is Lithuanian, as in the app.

Desktop (a wide window): the question, the picture and the Options on the left; a panel on the right with the buttons, and after Check the verdict and "why". Keys: 1–4 tick, Enter does the main action, N = "Nesu tikras". Use "Phone view" to see the phone.

Phone: one layout. After Check, your pick and the right answer stay above a short bottom bar with the verdict, the key sentence and "Toliau". The "why" follows below.

Try this

  1. Question 1 is a new Rule. Guess, or tap "Pirma išmokti" to read the Rule first.
  2. Answer one Question with "Nesu tikras" (not sure, key N on desktop): the explanation stays open. Answer sure and right: it folds away.
  3. Get Question 3 (U-turns) wrong. It comes back two Questions later with other Options.
  4. Open the Mock Exam: skip, go back, finish early, try "Be laiko ribos", or fast-forward the timer.

The Lesson end and Mock Exam results screens are placeholders: they are designed during the build.
