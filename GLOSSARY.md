# KETGO

A web service that prepares people for the Regitra theory exam, aiming to win learners from existing KET sites through smarter study and a more pleasant, mobile-friendly experience.

## Exam

**Theory Exam**:
The real Regitra theoretical driving exam a learner is preparing for.
_Avoid_: test, ticket, bilietas (when you mean the real exam)

**License Category**:
The class of driving licence an exam is for (A, B, C, D…). KETGO launches with category B only.
_Avoid_: type, class

## Content

**Question**:
A stem (text, usually with an image) plus up to four Options, of which one or more are correct.
_Avoid_: ticket, item

**Option**:
One of the choices offered on a Question; it is either correct or not.
_Avoid_: answer (ambiguous with the learner's act of answering)

**Own Question**:
A Question written by the KETGO team. Only Own Questions are ever shown to learners.
_Avoid_: our questions, KETGO bank

**Reference Bank**:
The exported ketbilietai.lt question database, used only to spot topics our Own Questions don't cover yet. Never shown to learners and never reworded into Own Questions.
_Avoid_: question bank, ketbilietai questions (when you mean content we serve)

## Growth

**Partner**:
A driving school or content creator who sends learners to KETGO through their own personal link or promo code.
_Avoid_: affiliate, reseller
