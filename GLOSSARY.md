# KETGO

A web service that prepares people for the Regitra theory exam, aiming to win learners from existing KET sites through smarter study and a more pleasant, mobile-friendly experience.

## Exam

**Theory Exam**:
The real Regitra theoretical driving exam a learner is preparing for.
_Avoid_: test, ticket, bilietas (when you mean the real exam)

**License Category**:
The class of driving licence an exam is for (A, B, C, D…). KETGO launches with category B only.
_Avoid_: type, class

**Theme**:
One of the nine subject areas the Theory Exam draws its Questions from, each with a fixed number of Questions per exam.
_Avoid_: topic, tema, chapter

## Content

**Rule**:
One thing a learner must know to answer a Question correctly, such as the right-hand rule or what sign 204 STOP means. Grounded in one or more KET points, but not the same as a KET point.
_Avoid_: KET point, topic (when you mean this unit)

**Question**:
A stem (text, usually with an image) plus the Options written for it, of which one or more are correct. Each Question has one main Rule. Each time it is shown, up to four of its Options are drawn: a Question with one correct Option always shows it, plus Distractors from its Distractor Pool; a Question with several correct Options also draws from those, so how many shown Options are correct (one to all) can change between showings.
_Avoid_: ticket, item

**Option**:
One of the choices offered on a Question; it is either correct or not.
_Avoid_: answer (ambiguous with the learner's act of answering)

**Distractor**:
An Option that is not correct.
_Avoid_: wrong answer

**Distractor Pool**:
All the Distractors written for one Question. Each time the Question is shown, it draws a different mix from its pool, so learners recognise the rule rather than the look of the answer.
_Avoid_: answer variants

**Own Question**:
A Question written by the KETGO team. Only Own Questions are ever shown to learners.
_Avoid_: our questions, KETGO bank

**Reference Bank**:
The exported ketbilietai.lt question database, used only to spot topics our Own Questions don't cover yet. Never shown to learners and never reworded into Own Questions.
_Avoid_: question bank, ketbilietai questions (when you mean content we serve)

## Learning

**Program**:
A learner's route through every Rule: a numbered sequence of Lessons in a curated order, taken at the learner's own pace. Reviews mix every Rule met so far.
_Avoid_: plan, course, path

**Lesson**:
One numbered, named step of the Program: a small group of related new Rules, each met first as a Question and then taught, with due Rules mixed in.
_Avoid_: unit, level, day, session

**Review**:
A short round of Questions on Rules that are due again, so they are not forgotten.
_Avoid_: repeat, revision, mistakes mode

**Mock Exam**:
A practice run of the Theory Exam under the same conditions, with feedback only at the end.
_Avoid_: test, ticket, bilietas, simulation

**Remembered**:
The state of a Rule the learner is predicted to still recall on their exam day. Answering it right once or twice is not enough; a miss drops it back to learning.
_Avoid_: learned, mastered, done

**Memory Hook**:
A short trick for remembering one Rule: an image, a rhyme, or a "think of it as…".
_Avoid_: mnemonic, tip (too vague)

**Exam Trap**:
A warning about wording or detail that commonly catches learners out in the Theory Exam.
_Avoid_: gotcha, tip (too vague)

**Rule Explainer**:
A short piece that teaches one Rule.
_Avoid_: article, theory page

## Learners

**Learner**:
A person preparing for the Theory Exam with KETGO. A Learner exists from their first tap on Start, with or without an Account.
_Avoid_: user, student, guest

**Account**:
An email address attached to a Learner, so their progress is safe and can be opened on another device.
_Avoid_: profile, registration, login

## Growth

**Partner**:
A driving school or content creator who sends learners to KETGO through their own personal link or promo code.
_Avoid_: affiliate, reseller
