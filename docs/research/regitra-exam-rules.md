# Regitra category-B Theory Exam rules (2026)

Research for issue [#2](https://github.com/Whirlywack/ketgo-web/issues/2). Checked on 2026-10-13.

Words like **Theory Exam**, **Question** and **Option** mean what `GLOSSARY.md` says.

## Short answer

| Topic | Answer | Main source |

## Details

### 1. Number of Questions, time, pass mark

- **30 Questions.** "B1, B arba BE kategorijų transporto priemonių testą sudaro 30 bendrųjų temų klausimų." (*The test for B1, B or BE is made of 30 Questions from the general themes.*) Source: 1V-115, part A, **p. 9**. Regitra's Lithuanian page says the same under "Teorijos egzamino vertinimas", point 1.
- **Questions are picked at random** by computer: 1V-329, **p. 24**. The test is "su pasirenkamais atsakymais" (multiple choice).
- **Up to 2 extra trial Questions.** "Testas gali būti papildomas 2 naujai parengtais klausimais, jei reikia patikrinti naujų klausimų kokybę. Šiuo atveju atsakymai į naujus klausimus nevertinami." (*The test can get 2 new Questions to check their quality. Answers to them are not scored.*) Source: 1V-115, part A, **p. 14**.
  - **UNCONFIRMED:** whether this really happens, whether the screen then shows 32 Questions and 32 minutes, and whether you can see which Questions are the trial ones. The demo always showed 30.
- **Time.** "…testui spręsti skiriama tiek minučių, kiek teste yra klausimų" (*you get as many minutes as there are Questions*). Source: 1V-115, part B, **p. 8**. For category B this is 30 minutes. The demo timer starts at 00:30:00 ("Laiko liko").
- **Pass mark.** "Egzaminas laikomas išlaikytu, jei per skirtą laiką teisingai atsakyta į ne mažiau kaip 80 proc. klausimų" (*passed if at least 80 % of the Questions are answered correctly in the time given*). Source: 1V-115, part B, **p. 8**. 80 % of 30 is 24. The demo result screen shows "Leistinas klaidų kiekis: 6" (*allowed mistakes: 6*).
- **After the exam.** You see the result on the screen in the exam room. Sources: 1V-329, **p. 28.1** and **p. 33**. A pass is valid until you pass the driving test, for at most 1 year: 1V-329, **p. 26**. After a fail you can retake after at least 7 days: 1V-329, **p. 7**.

### 2. Questions per theme

Source: 1V-115, part A, annex "TEORIJOS EGZAMINO KLAUSIMŲ SUDARYMO SCHEMA", table 1 "Bendrosios temos", column "Egzamino metu pateikiamų klausimų skaičius". The English theme names come from Regitra's own file ["General topics" (xlsx)](https://www.regitra.lt/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/General-topics.xlsx).

| # | Theme (Regitra's English name) | Questions | Share | Annex 2 content points of 1V-329 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Awareness of the risks of driving a motor vehicle | **6** | 20 % | 1.2–1.9 |
| 2 | Rules of conduct when driving on public roads | **5** | 17 % | 1.1 |
| 3 | Driving priority and giving way | **3** | 10 % | 1.1, 1.13 |
| 4 | Special roads and road sections, road users and manoeuvres | **3** | 10 % | 1.1 |
| 5 | Safe driving and responding to critical situations | **3** | 10 % | 1.1, 1.11, 1.15 |
| 6 | Road markings, traffic signs, traffic lights and traffic controllers | **4** | 13 % | 1.1 |
| 7 | Responsible, environment-aware driving | **2** | 7 % | 1.2, 1.16 |
| 8 | General rules of the KET and other road-traffic laws | **2** | 7 % | 1.1, 1.10 |
| 9 | Knowledge of the vehicle | **2** | 7 % | 1.1, 1.12, 1.14 |
| | **Total** | **30** | | |

**Our two notes compared with the law:**

- Note A, 6/5/**4**/3/3/**3**/2/2/2: these are the right numbers, but in the wrong order. Themes 3 and 6 are swapped (it looks like the list was sorted from big to small). Priority (theme 3) gets **3**, not 4. Signs and markings (theme 6) get **4**, not 3.
- Note B, 6/**4**/**4**/3/3/4/2/2/2: this is wrong for theme 2 (5, not 4) and theme 3 (3, not 4).
- **The right order is 6/5/3/3/3/4/2/2/2.** It is the same in the 2021 original and in the 2025 text.

The annex also names a "knowledge level" for each theme:

- **Zn**: remember a rule or a term.
- **Tk**: apply the rules to a situation.
- **Sv**: see what matters on the road.
- **Sp**: understand the risk and the results of what road users do.

Theme 1 is mostly Sp/Sv, themes 2–4 and 6–7 are Sv, themes 5 and 8 are Tk, and theme 9 is Zn. Source: 1V-115, part A, **p. 6** and the annex.

### 3. Questions with several correct Options

- **Scoring is all or nothing.** Source: 1V-115, part B, **p. 8.1–8.2**.
  - 8.1: "klausimas laikomas teisingai atsakytu, jei į klausimą egzaminuojamasis pateikė visus teisingus atsakymus (kai tokių buvo ne vienas)" (*a Question is correct if you gave all the correct answers, when there is more than one*).
  - 8.2: "…neteisingai atsakytu, jei egzaminuojamasis pažymėjo ne visus teisingus atsakymus ir (arba) pažymėjo bent vieną klaidingą atsakymą" (*it is wrong if you did not tick all the correct answers and/or ticked at least one wrong answer*).
- **The demo shows the same.** In demo test 284, **4 of 30 Questions had more than one correct Option**: 2 of 4, 2 of 4, 3 of 4, and one where **all 3 Options were correct**. On 3 of them we ticked one correct Option only, and the review marked each one wrong.
- **You are not told how many to tick (demo).**
  - Every Option has a checkbox, also on Questions with only one correct Option. You can tick several Options on any Question.
  - The screen never says "choose 1" or "choose 2". We checked all screen labels on all 30 demo Questions, in Lithuanian and in English. The only labels are the navigation, the timer and "no illustration".
  - So the learner must judge each Option on its own.
  - The law does not say whether the number must be shown. **UNCONFIRMED** for the real exam, but 1V-329 p. 23 says the point of the familiarisation tool is to show how the exam program works (see section 4).
- **The demo's review screen**, after you finish, marks correct Options green and wrong ones red. It also has a link "Klausimo pagrindimas" (*why this is the answer*). The law does not say whether the real exam shows a review. 1V-329 p. 28.1 only says you can see the test assessment ("testo įvertinimas") on the screen. **UNCONFIRMED.**

### 4. Skipping, going back, changing answers

The law does not describe the screen. It only says: "Egzaminuojamiesiems sudaroma galimybė prieš teorijos egzaminą susipažinti su egzaminavimo programos veikimo principu" (*candidates can learn how the exam program works before the exam*). Source: 1V-329, **p. 23**. Regitra's way of doing this is the public demo, so we take the demo as the best primary evidence. What we saw (2026-10-13):

- **Skip:** "Kitas klausimas" (*Next*) works without an answer.
- **Go back:** "Ankstesnis klausimas" (*Previous*) works from Question 2 on. A bar with buttons 1–30 jumps to any Question. Answered Questions are marked in the bar.
- **Change:** when you come back, your ticks are still there, and you can untick or tick again. Nothing locks.
- **Finish:** the "Užbaigti testą" (*Finish test*) button is visible only on Question 30. If some Questions are empty, a box says: "Dėmesio! Yra neatsakytų klausimų. Jie automatiškai bus pažymėti kaip neteisingai atsakyti klausimai. Ar norite baigti testą?" (*Unanswered Questions will be marked as wrong. Finish?*) If all are answered, it only asks "Ar norite baigti testą?".
- **Time out:** the law counts only answers given "per skirtą laiką" (*within the time given*), 1V-115 part B p. 8. **UNCONFIRMED:** whether the screen submits by itself at 0:00 or locks. We started a demo run to find out but stopped it before the timer ran out. Given the "unanswered = wrong" message above, empty Questions most likely count as wrong.
- **Order:** in Lithuanian the Question order was the same every time. The English version has the same Questions in another order. The **order of the Options changed each time we loaded the demo**. In the real exam, Questions are picked at random for each candidate (1V-329, p. 24).
- **Result screen:** shows "Egzaminas išlaikytas / neišlaikytas" (*passed / failed*), the number of Questions given, correct and wrong counts with %, and "Leistinas klaidų kiekis 6" (*allowed mistakes: 6*).

**UNCONFIRMED:** that the real exam room software works exactly like the demo. The demo is also out of date on languages (it still offers Russian), so it may differ in small ways.

### 5. Pictures

- **The law sets no share of picture Questions.** Neither act says how many Questions have pictures. **UNCONFIRMED** for the real Question pool, which is confidential (1V-115, part A, **p. 3**).
- **Demo test 284 (30 Questions):**

  | Kind | Count | Share |
  |---|---|---|
  | Computer-drawn (3D) road situation: driver's-eye or street-level view (10) or bird's-eye view of a crossing (3), with a Regitra watermark | 13 | 43 % |
  | Road-sign drawing on a white background | 7 | 23 % |
  | Real photo of a road, road marking or sign in a street | 4 | 13 % |
  | Real photo of something else (the exam car) | 1 | 3 % |
  | No picture (the box says "Iliustracijos nėra", *no illustration*) | 5 | 17 % |
  | **With a picture** | **25** | **83 %** |

- So more than half of the demo Questions (17 of 30, 57 %) show a **road situation** (drawn or photo), and about a quarter show **a sign alone**.
- The picture is always one image above the Question text, in a box about 500 × 300 px. Options are text only. There were no video or sound Questions.
- **Caveat:** this is one fixed test of 30. It is a hint about the real mix, not a measurement.

### 6. Languages

- **The law:**
  - From 1 Jan 2026: "Teorijos egzaminas laikomas valstybine ir, jeigu yra galimybė, kitomis oficialiomis Europos Sąjungos kalbomis." (*The Theory Exam is in the state language and, when possible, in other official EU languages.*) Source: 1V-329, **p. 4**, second paragraph (change made by 1V-768 of 19 Dec 2024).
  - A third paragraph lets **Ukrainian citizens** take the Theory Exam in Ukrainian while their temporary-protection period lasts. Source: 1V-329, **p. 4** (changed by 1V-104 of 6 Feb 2026).
- **Regitra's pages:**
  - The English page says: "From January 1, 2026, the theory exam can be taken in Lithuanian, English, Polish, Spanish and French."
  - The Lithuanian page lists **lietuvių, anglų, lenkų, ispanų, prancūzų, vokiečių, ukrainiečių** (Lithuanian, English, Polish, Spanish, French, German, Ukrainian). You choose the language when you register.
- **Russian is gone.** The 29 Sep 2025 news says Russian is dropped from 1 Jan 2026, and that German is planned for later. In 2024, 9.7 % of Theory Exams were in Russian and 2.5 % in English.
- **Ukrainian** started on **9 Feb 2026** (news of 9 Feb 2026). That news also gives January 2026 numbers: Lithuanian 91.7 %, English 7.5 %, Polish 0.6 %, French and Spanish under 0.1 % each.
- **German:** **UNCONFIRMED** start date. It is listed on the Lithuanian page, but it was still "planned" in the Sep 2025 news, and we found no news saying it started.
- **Interpreter:** only for people with a sensory or medical reason. The interpreter translates only from Lithuanian, and the test can get up to 15 extra minutes. Source: Regitra English page, "In cases where interpreter services are necessary"; 1V-329, p. 4.
- **The demo** offers only Lithuanian, English and Russian. It is out of date.

### 7. Other facts that may matter for KETGO

- **Exam Questions are secret.**
  - They "yra konfidencialūs ir negali būti viešinami" (*are confidential and may not be published*). Source: 1V-115, part A, **p. 3**.
  - Copying or spreading them is forbidden, and Regitra can claim damages. Source: 1V-329, **p. 31**.
  - This supports the rule that only Own Questions are shown to learners. Our demo notes keep only counts, never Question text.
- Regitra writes and approves the Questions (1V-329, **p. 25**). The content list in 1V-329 **Annex 2, points 1.1–1.16** is the official syllabus for the general themes.
- The Theory Exam is taken on a computer and is filmed with sound (1V-329, **p. 22–23**).

## What this means for a KETGO mock Theory Exam (input for other tickets)

- 30 Questions, 30-minute timer, pass at 24 correct (max 6 mistakes).
- Draw Questions per theme in the order 6/5/3/3/3/4/2/2/2.
- Always use checkboxes. Never show "choose N". Score all or nothing. Shuffle the Options.
- Allow skip, back, jump by number, and changing answers. Put "Finish" on the last Question, with a warning about empty Questions. Empty Questions count as wrong. When the timer ends, auto-submit (our choice; Regitra's exact behaviour is unconfirmed).
- About 4 of 5 Questions should have a picture, and over half should show a road situation, not just a sign. This is from one demo test, so treat it as a rough goal.
